the premise

What you've been calling anxiety may be overstimulation.

By mid-morning you've met more inputs than your grandparents did in a week. The mind copes the only way it can: it goes on autopilot. The days keep happening. You just stop being in them.

This week turns the volume back down. Seven days, one small practice at a time, together.

day one · your host

Cory opens the week.

You know his voice from The Astral Hustle and his book Now Is the Way. For a decade he has asked the clearest teachers alive one question: how do you actually stay present? This week begins where his answer begins. The problem is volume, and volume can be lowered.

He opens the week on day one and hosts its first live call. Thirty minutes, everyone welcome to speak.
